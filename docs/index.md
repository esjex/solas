# Siobhan Fitzgerald-Gibson

**Technical Author, UX Writer, Content Strategist**

Hello! I'm an experienced technical communicator, available to help you turn complex information into clear, human-readable content.

I have over a decade of experience working on projects of all sizes, from major development software like Unity Technologies to small start-up tools. I also have experience leading teams and projects, establishing documentation quality standards, mentoring technical writers, and speaking at international conferences.

I work through my consultancy, Solas Documentation Ltd. I'm especially drawn to projects with a public-interest angle: civic tech, public sector, and anything where **good documentation genuinely makes people's lives easier**.

## Areas of expertise

I'm particularly good at owning and producing these things: 

- **Documentation**: User manuals, API references, and docs tools
- **Learning materials**: Tutorials and walkthroughs
- **Community content**: Technical articles and marketing content
- **User interfaces**: UI text and user journeys
- **Internal communication**: Technical docs and processes to keep teams connected

## Information for good

Information is empowering. Beyond my client work, I contribute docs and communications support to hobby projects and community events. I also write zines and resources, provide healthcare advocacy, volunteer at games and maker/hacker festivals, and teach music in a local samba band.

Subject to conditions, I can offer a lower rate on projects and consultations for small businesses, non-profit organisations, and social causes.
