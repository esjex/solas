# Skiller Whale: Designing programmer training courses for engineers

**Designing a live-coached course on pair programming**  
*Freelance instructional designer · course research, structure, and writing*

Researched, structured, and wrote a live-coached workshop that turns pair programming from an intimidating idea into something engineers feel ready to try, now a standing part of Skiller Whale's course catalogue.

### The problem

Skiller Whale is an e-learning provider that trains software engineers at all levels through live tech coaching and micro-workshops. The client wanted a new course on pair programming, with a goal of getting more engineers comfortable enough to actually try pairing in their own workplaces. The course didn’t only focus on technical knowledge; it also needed to boost confidence and lower a social and behavioural barrier.

### The goal

Research, structure, and write a live-coached workshop on pair programming that would leave engineers ready to try it themselves.

### Approach

I worked alongside a subject matter expert who provided the technical expertise while I led the research, structure, and writing. It was a genuine division of labour: her subject knowledge, my instructional design.

* **Started from the client's research**, and talked through a general shape with the SME before doing my own research to deepen and pressure-test it.  
* **Designed the course on information-architecture principles**, sequencing it so each idea rested on the last: what pairing *is*, then how it works, then the social models (peer pairing, mixed-skill pairing), then the working styles (fixed driver-navigator, ping-pong), then its specific application in TDD, and finally the common challenges and how to overcome them.  
* **Treated it as a communication course as much as a technical one**. The structure deliberately gave as much weight to the interpersonal dynamics of pairing as to the mechanics, understanding that the users are human, and good social cohesion is essential to adoption.  
* **Designed discussion questions and talking points**, turning it from a lecture into something a coach could actively facilitate in a live session.  
* **Delivered the modules via GitHub**, ready for the SME to run.

### Outcome

Skiller Whale now has a Programming Skills section in its module list, anchored by a 90-minute live-coached Pair Programming workshop delivered by the SME I worked alongside.
